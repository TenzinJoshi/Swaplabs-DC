Detailed idea submission

Give the community enough substance to help.

A title gets attention. A clear problem, proposed solution, impact claim, funding breakdown, support request, and next experiment earn useful engagement.

Publish your idea

Your submission becomes visible to the community after it is saved.

Your profile is shown as the idea owner. Private profiles still reveal only their permitted basic identity until a follow request is accepted.

Separate evidence from belief

State what you know, how you know it, what you infer, and what still needs testing. Honest uncertainty attracts better collaborators.

Make the funding ask inspectable

Explain the next milestone, the cost drivers, and what a successful use of funds would produce. Avoid guaranteed return claims.

Center affected people

Do not design for a community without a plan to involve them in decisions, research, testing, consent, and ownership.

Protect safety and rights

Consider privacy, accessibility, bias, environmental effects, safeguarding, legal obligations, and ways the idea could be misused.

From idea to reality

Recognition grows from evidence and responsible progress.

The Lab is designed to help an early idea become easier to understand, test, support, and trust.

Discover

Understand the real problem

Speak with affected people, inspect existing attempts, and identify the decision or behavior that must change.

Frame

Make the idea legible

Explain the mechanism, beneficiary, evidence, risks, funding, and precise support request in plain language.

Test

Run the smallest honest experiment

Choose a test that can disprove a key assumption before spending more money or making larger claims.

Build

Grow with accountable partners

Use skills, comments, pilots, recognition, and funding to deepen evidence while keeping affected communities involved.
