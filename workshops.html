Designed for participation

Four formats for four kinds of progress.

The format tells you how the room will work before you reserve a place.

Live online

Guided from anywhere

A host demonstrates, participants practice, and the room pauses at planned checkpoints for questions and review.

Small group

Focused peer feedback

Bring a draft, attempt, or decision. Structured turns ensure every participant receives useful attention.

Practice circle

Build confidence through repetition

Ideal for languages, presentations, interviews, and other skills that improve by doing them with supportive people.

Hybrid

Connect local and remote rooms

Join at a community venue or online, with one shared brief and accessible materials for both groups.

Before you join

Know exactly how to arrive prepared.

Workshop reminders include the host, room, expected level, equipment, and any optional preparation.

Confirmation and reminder

Your saved registration appears immediately and creates a notification. A workshop reference stays connected to the administration record.

Cancel responsibly

If plans change, cancel from the schedule. The place returns to the live seat count for another learner.

Share access needs

Contact support before the session for captions, pacing, materials, or participation adjustments.

Keep the outcome

Save your finished artifact, reflection, or next step in your own learning record after the room closes.

Host a rigorous workshop

Turn one skill into an experience people can complete.

Start with one outcome

“Participants will leave with a revised portfolio story” is clearer than “participants will understand portfolios.”

Protect the room size

Choose the smallest seat limit that still makes the activity work. More places are not always more useful.

Design for safety

Use inclusive examples, clear boundaries, accessible materials, and a realistic plan for questions you cannot answer.

Experienced members can apply to host workshops and structured learning rooms.
