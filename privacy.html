Privacy policy

Your information should stay understandable and under your control.

This policy explains what SwapLabs collects, why it is used, how visibility works, and how you can manage or delete information. Last updated September 29, 2026.

1. Scope and privacy choices

This policy applies to the SwapLabs website, account features, profile and portfolio surfaces, matching, messages, calendar, sessions, credits, learning records, support forms, and related pages. It does not control third-party sites linked from SwapLabs, including Google, calendar providers, browsers, or social platforms. Review those providers’ own privacy notices before using them.

SwapLabs is designed to collect only what is useful for the feature being used. Some fields are required for account security or matching; optional fields can be left blank. You can adjust available visibility, communication, analytics, retention, and account controls from your profile and settings. A setting may not undo information another person already copied or information that must be kept for security, disputes, or law.

2. Information we collect

Account and identity information

We may collect a display name, username, email address, phone number if supplied, password-derived authentication data, date of birth or age-related information, country, timezone, language, guardian details where applicable, avatar presentation settings, and account status. We do not need your password in readable form to operate the account.

Profile and community information

We collect skills you can teach, skills you want to learn, goals, experience, availability, session preferences, portfolio content, ideas, feedback, group activity, follows, reports, and visibility choices. What other members can see depends on the feature and your selected privacy settings.

Activity and transaction information

We record sign-ins, profile changes, matches, conversations, notifications, calendar events, workshop participation, calls’ room and attendance state, learning-journal entries, streak sessions and freezes, achievements, time-credit ledger entries, payments, disputes, refunds, gifts, and administrator actions. These records help us provide the feature, protect members, and keep an accountable history.

Device, log, and preference information

Requests may include IP address, approximate technical location, browser and device details, timestamps, referring page, error information, cookies, session identifiers, and security tokens. Your browser may also store preferences such as theme, navigation density, saved interface state, and locally cached account hints. Local storage belongs to that browser and device; it is not a substitute for the account record.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate local sign-ins; display profiles and portfolios according to settings; calculate explainable matches; send messages, notifications, reminders, and support replies; create and update calendar records; operate workshops, calls, gifts, credits, achievements, and streaks; prevent abuse; investigate reports and disputes; troubleshoot failures; measure product usage; improve accessibility and reliability; comply with legal obligations; and enforce the Terms and Conditions.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information for planning and reporting when it cannot reasonably be used to identify a person. We do not use a match score, streak, badge, public count, or automated notice as a guarantee of identity, expertise, safety, educational outcome, or eligibility.

4. Visibility, sharing, and member control

Information you mark public may be visible to other members or visitors, including profile identity, selected skills, portfolio items, public projects, public feedback, or community contributions. Private profile fields, direct messages, account records, precise location, guardian information, and credit history are restricted to the people or administrators authorized for that feature.

We share information only when reasonably needed to provide a feature, protect people or the service, follow your instruction, or comply with law. Examples include sharing a calendar event with its participants, a message with conversation participants, a workshop registration with its host, a report with authorized reviewers, or a public profile with the audience you selected. We do not sell personal information or use private messages to build an advertising profile.

Members can copy or screenshot information they are permitted to view. SwapLabs cannot control a recipient’s later use of a lawful view, so share only what you are comfortable making available to that audience.

5. Local storage, cookies, and optional links

This SwapLabs version runs entirely on localhost. The application, SQLite database, uploaded files, and study-room connections stay on this computer. Cloudflare’s development runtime simulates the database and file-storage interfaces locally; it does not use a hosted SwapLabs database.

Accounts use local sign-in. Google sign-in and external email delivery are unavailable in this local version. Account recovery displays a verification code locally after the saved account details match.

The local application does not load Google Analytics, external tracking scripts, hosted fonts, or hosted icons. All interface assets are included with the project.

Cookies and local storage may remember a signed-in session, security state, theme, navigation preferences, and other functional choices. Clearing them can sign you out or reset preferences. Optional links to social networks, learning resources, and calendar providers open their own websites only when you choose them.

6. Messages, calls, uploads, and safety review

Direct messages and attachments are available to the participants authorized for that conversation, subject to moderation and security controls. A valid report, safeguarding concern, fraud investigation, or legal request may allow authorized administrators to review relevant content and account records. We may restrict delivery, remove material, preserve evidence, or disclose information where reasonably necessary to prevent harm or comply with law.

Video rooms use browser permissions and WebRTC. Camera, microphone, and screen media are exchanged by participating browsers and are not intentionally recorded by SwapLabs as a normal room feature. We do store room permissions, invitations, waiting-room status, connection-signaling data, and attendance or completion records needed to operate the room and resolve disputes. Do not record another participant without appropriate consent.

Uploads are subject to file-type, size, access, safety, and retention controls. No online service is completely secure. Protect your credentials, use a unique password, avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information, and report suspected compromise promptly.

7. Young people and guardians

SwapLabs may use age or date-of-birth information to apply additional safeguards. Where required, a parent or guardian must provide consent or review account use. Student features may limit profile visibility, direct contact, calls, public submissions, or access to sensitive community features. Guardian details and review outcomes are restricted account records.

These controls reduce risk but do not prove identity or guarantee a safe interaction. Adults should supervise young people’s use where appropriate. If you believe a child’s information was submitted improperly or a child is at risk, contact SwapLabs through the Contact page and use local emergency services for immediate danger.

8. Retention, account export, and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, complete a transaction history, handle reports and disputes, comply with legal duties, or resolve conflicts. Retention varies by category and by your available settings. Backups, audit records, fraud signals, ledger entries, and safety records may persist for a limited additional period or be retained in restricted or de-identified form.

Available account controls may let you export account information, download session history, reduce retention, clear selected records, correct profile details, or delete your account. Deleting an account does not necessarily delete information that another member is entitled to retain in a conversation or shared event, or records needed for security, legal, credit, dispute, or audit purposes. We will not retain information longer than reasonably necessary for those purposes.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for certain processing, and to complain to a relevant data-protection authority. We may need to verify your request and may apply lawful exceptions for another person’s privacy, security, legal claims, fraud prevention, or record integrity. We do not discriminate against you for making a lawful privacy request.

To request access, correction, deletion, or clarification, use the Contact & support page and identify the account email or username. Never send a password, Google secret, administrator code, or full payment credentials in a contact request.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers, legal obligations, or privacy practices change. The new version will show a new “Last updated” date, and we will provide additional notice where required or reasonably practical. If you continue using SwapLabs after the effective date, the updated policy applies.

This policy is a plain-language product notice, not legal advice. If you need advice about rights, consent, international transfers, or a regulated activity, consult a qualified professional in your jurisdiction. Privacy questions and complaints can be sent through Contact & support.
