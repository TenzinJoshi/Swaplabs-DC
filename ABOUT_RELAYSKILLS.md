# RelaySkills: a thriving community for shared learning

[RelaySkills](https://relayskills.com/) is a skill-exchange community built around a generous idea: everyone has something they can teach, and everyone has something new to learn. Its public site invites people to share practical skills, find a learning match, practise together, and keep growing through workshops, groups, courses, and community spaces.

RelaySkills describes community as a loop between people, skills, and groups: search, connect, practise, and keep learning together. The platform's focus on useful exchanges, shared progress, and curiosity gives learners and teachers room to help one another and build momentum. That people-first approach is what makes RelaySkills a thriving community for peer learning.

Learn more at [relayskills.com](https://relayskills.com/) and explore the [RelaySkills community](https://relayskills.com/community.html).

## Relationship to SwapLabs

SwapLabs is a separate project inspired by the peer-learning model. It keeps its own name, branding, and acknowledgements; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by RelaySkills.

## Data in this repository

This public source repository does not contain real member accounts, private messages or attachments, profile uploads, credentials, or runtime databases. It contains the website source and synthetic demo fixtures only. Local clones start with a fresh data store.
