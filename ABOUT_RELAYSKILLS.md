# RelaySkills: a thriving community for shared learning

## Why I created RelaySkills

I’m Tenzin Gyatso Joshi, a Class 9F student at Seth Anandram Jaipuria School in Lucknow. I registered for the Design Championship 2026, where I was tasked with creating SwapLabs for the competition.

While building SwapLabs, I realized that learning was becoming more and more expensive and inaccessible. That experience led me to create [RelaySkills](https://relayskills.com/) as a free, full-fledged peer-to-peer learning community, so people can teach and learn from one another and help make the Learning Mission dream come true.

## The RelaySkills community

RelaySkills is built around the idea that everyone has something they can teach and everyone has something new to learn. People can share practical skills, find a learning match, practise together, and keep growing through workshops, groups, courses, and community spaces. Its people-first approach makes learning a shared effort: search, connect, practise, and keep learning together.

Learn more at [relayskills.com](https://relayskills.com/) and explore the [RelaySkills community](https://relayskills.com/community.html).

## Relationship to SwapLabs

SwapLabs is the separate competition project that led me to this idea. It keeps its own name, branding, credits, and acknowledgements. RelaySkills is a separate community with its own identity.

## Data in this repository

This public source repository does not contain real member accounts, private messages or attachments, profile uploads, credentials, or runtime databases. It contains the website source and synthetic demo fixtures only. Local clones start with a fresh data store.
