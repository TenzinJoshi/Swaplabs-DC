What each recommendation considers

Compatibility is recalculated from live account data whenever you search. Your secondary languages count as skills you can teach.

Grouped skill fitPrimary factor

Exact matches rise first, followed by skills in the same family such as web development, design, fitness, or languages. A reciprocal offer is helpful but never required.

Goals & interestsContext boost

Shared goals and interests help surface people who are likely to make the first conversation useful.

Teaching styleCompatibility boost

Project, structure, conversation, practice, or flexibility.

Session formatCompatibility boost

Remote, local, individual, group, or flexible sessions.

Schedule & timezoneUp to 8 points

Shared weekly windows after timezone conversion.

LanguagesUp to 8 points

Primary and additional languages can appear as teachable skills and shared session languages.

Experience levelUp to 5 points

Whether the teacher’s listed level supports yours.

Exchange historyUp to 6 points

Completed sessions and member ratings, with a fair new-user state.

Optional preferences0–12 points

Only when enabled by you, broad age-band and gender preferences can refine the order. They never block a member or reveal private details.
